# AssetWatch

> AssetWatch is a predictive maintenance and condition monitoring software platform that combines wireless IoT sensors, AI-powered analytics, and expert human oversight to help industrial manufacturers prevent unplanned downtime and extend equipment life.

AssetWatch serves maintenance and reliability teams across heavy industry. The platform monitors rotating equipment in real time — detecting early signs of failure in motors, pumps, fans, gearboxes, compressors, and more — and delivers prioritized alerts with recommended actions. Services include continuous vibration monitoring, route-based vibration analysis, oil analysis, and CMMS integration.

## Core Product & Platform

- [Continuous Vibration Monitoring](https://www.assetwatch.com/solutions/vibration-continuous): Wireless, always-on vibration monitoring for rotating assets
- [Route-Based Vibration Analysis](https://www.assetwatch.com/solutions/vibration-route-based-analysis): Periodic vibration data collection and expert analysis
- [Oil Analysis](https://www.assetwatch.com/solutions/oil-analysis): Lubricant testing and fluid health monitoring to detect wear and contamination
- [CMMS Integration](https://www.assetwatch.com/solutions/cmms): Connecting condition monitoring alerts directly to work order workflows
